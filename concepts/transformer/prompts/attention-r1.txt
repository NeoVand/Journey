Concept art for one scene of an interactive 3D website that explains attention in a transformer language model. Use the reference images for WHAT is shown (word tiles on a map-like layer, arcs from one word back to earlier words with different strengths), but completely restyle it so it feels refined, clean and premium, like a scientific visualization or a jewel-like museum piece, never plasticky.

The scene: one thin frosted-glass plate floating in deep navy space, seen from slightly above. On it, a single row of fourteen small glowing beads, one per word of a sentence, resting on a faint pastel watercolour map printed into the glass. From one bead near the end of the row (the word being read), delicate arcs of light reach back to each earlier bead: each arc's brightness and thickness shows how much attention it pays, two arcs clearly strongest and warm gold, the rest faint cobalt hairlines. The beads after the reading word are dim and unlit, because a word can only look back. Beneath the plate, the same beads continue as fine vertical filaments down to a plate below. Colours: deep navy, frosted glass, soft gold, cobalt, a little coral. Subtle depth of field, gentle glow only on the arcs, no thick tubes, no chunky blocks, no text or letters.

Elegant, precise, calm. Leave the left third of the frame dark and calm for text.
