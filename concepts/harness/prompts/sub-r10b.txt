Concept art for one card of an interactive 3D explainer website: a close-up of one part of the room in the second reference (same materials, light and style; the first reference is the site's agreed art direction). The part: the small miniature room in the middle of the front of the floor, a subagent: a copy of the whole room in small, with low walls, its own tiny tray, its own glass tube and chute, its own tiny gate and benches. Inside it, at its front middle, a smaller copy of the same room, and inside that, a still smaller one: three rooms, one inside the next, and the smallest has no room inside it.

The moment: an amber card (the brief) has just gone in through a small door in the first room's right side, off the brass rail where the little amber cart waits. Inside the first room its own tiny loop is running: a tiny capsule rising in its tube, a few tiles in its tray. Coming back out of the door onto the cart: one single orange block (the summary), the only thing that leaves. In the background, softly out of focus, the big room's long tray along the back wall.
