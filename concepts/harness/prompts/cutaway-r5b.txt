Concept art for one scene of an interactive 3D explainer website about how a coding agent works. The first reference is the agreed art direction for the whole site. The second is this scene today: the harness, the program you installed, drawn as a cream cabinet with a vented side, a ring on its roof and four labelled drawers, next to a laptop. The third is an earlier cut-away we liked for its idea but found too busy and machine-like; the fourth is the object that must be at its heart now. Make it friendlier and calmer than the third: satin porcelain, cream keycap tiles, matte soft cables, small brass fittings, daylight, gentle shadows. No glowing tubes, no robot arms, no hazard stripes, no chevrons.

The same cabinet as today, its near side panel lifted away like a clean product cut-away, so we see that it holds one thing: a long, low porcelain tray running across its middle, front to back, with a firm end stop at the far end (the limit of what fits). The four drawers on its front slide straight into the tray's near end, and their contents are already in it as rounded keycap tiles in coloured runs, edge to edge, each run's length its size: pale wheat (the agent's instructions), a few pale sky-blue tiles (facts about your machine), a long caramel-tan run (the tools it may ask for, the biggest part), one cobalt tile (your prompt). Beyond them, the rest of the tray is empty, a little more than half of it. A frosted glass cover sits over the packed tiles.

Above the tray, the ring on the cabinet's roof is the way out: a small porcelain capsule carrying a copy of the tray is just rising through it toward the sky (sent to the model, far away). Low in the cabinet's back wall, three round ports with soft cables out to the laptop beside it: where the work on your machine will happen. A small porcelain filing drawer at the cabinet's foot keeps a copy of every tile. Nothing else inside: calm, almost empty, clearly one tray.

No text, letters or numbers anywhere. No people. Three-quarter view from slightly above, on a porcelain island in a sky-blue world with soft clouds. Keep the right third of the frame calm for a text card.
