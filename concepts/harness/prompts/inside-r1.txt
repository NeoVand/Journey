Concept art for one scene of an interactive 3D website that explains how a coding agent works. Match the look of the reference images exactly: glossy toy-like 3D objects with soft rounded bevels, porcelain-white floor, cream and ivory machines, cobalt-blue enamel, warm amber accents, jade green, brushed aluminum, soft warm studio light, gentle contact shadows, three-quarter isometric view from above, crisp product-render quality. No text anywhere. No people.

The subject: the inside of "the harness", the program on your laptop that runs the agent. We have stepped inside the cream cabinet and it is bigger on the inside: a warm cream-walled workshop, cut away so we see in, like a dollhouse. In the middle, a small round model-railway track forms a loop, and a little amber suitcase (the request) rides it on a tiny cart. Around the loop stand six charming, clearly different machines, each one acting out its job:
1. A packing table where four tiny objects are laid neatly into the open suitcase in a row: a small rulebook, a snow globe, a folded Swiss-army knife, a speech bubble, and a long paper scroll going in last.
2. A brass pneumatic tube rising through the ceiling, with a capsule whooshing up it.
3. A vintage ticker-tape teleprinter printing a long ribbon of tape that curls onto the floor.
4. A turnstile gate guarded by a small friendly robot bouncer holding a clipboard, with a green lamp, a silver desk bell and a red rubber stamp beside it.
5. A workbench with two mechanical arms reaching through a round porthole in the side wall into a filing cabinet full of files beyond it: one arm holds a magnifying glass, the other a pencil.
6. A typewriter feeding a very long paper scroll that runs across the floor into a filing drawer, next to a small desk with a stack of pages and a little press.
In one corner, a glass dome covers a miniature copy of the whole loop with an even tinier suitcase on it.

Everything is legible as an object, playful but elegant, not cluttered, with clear paths between machines. Composition leaves the left third of the frame calm for text.
