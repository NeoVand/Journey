Concept art for one card of an interactive 3D explainer website, a close-up of one station in the room shown in the second reference (same materials, light and style; the first reference is the site's agreed art direction). The station: the gate, a slim porcelain arch with a small round dome beacon on top, three small lamps (green, amber, red), a little brass bell, a round dial on one post (how much it may do alone), a slim rack of three small blank cards on the other post (your rules), and one soft cable plugged into its side running out of frame (your hooks). It stands in the floor groove between the reply chute (left, out of focus) and the three round brass ports in the low wall (right).

The moment: three amber cards (tool requests the model wrote) wait in a short queue in the groove before the gate. The gate decides each one:
- the first has passed through: the green lamp is lit and it glides on toward the ports;
- the second is held under the arch: the amber lamp is lit and the little bell is ringing (tiny motion lines), and on the laptop just outside the wall a small dialog card with two rounded buttons has appeared: the person is being asked;
- the third has been turned back: the red lamp lit, and the card has become a small red tile sliding back toward the tray, away from the ports: refused, it never runs.
Close three-quarter view from above, the gate in the centre right, shallow depth of field, calm warm daylight, satin porcelain and brass, no glow. The left third of the frame is calm and soft for a text card. No text, letters or numbers anywhere. No people.
