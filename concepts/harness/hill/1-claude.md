# Round 1 (Claude): memory, hands and reins

## The description, in one breath

> The model is far away and can only read and write. The harness is everything else: its **memory**, its **hands**
> and your **reins**. Every turn it hands the model one tray of everything so far; when the reply asks for something,
> the reins decide, the hands do it on your machine, and what happened goes into the tray. Round and round, until a
> reply asks for nothing.

Why it works: three plain words a newcomer keeps, each one a real part, and the word "harness" means exactly this for
a horse (it puts a strong animal's pull to work, and gives the driver the reins). The cards can say that once.

## Three zones, one loop

| Zone | Real thing | Objects | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Memory** | the context, rebuilt and reread every turn; the record on disk | the tray (hero) with its end stop; the four drawers that fill its start; the skill shelf; the writer at the open end and its slot to disk; the press | the back of the room |
| **Reins** | permission: your rules, the mode, asking you, your hooks | the gate (rule rack, mode dial, bell), your screen's window, the hook port | the left, by you |
| **Hands** | tools running on your machine; the subagent | three benches with ports to your files, your shell (fence when sandboxed) and an MCP server; the subagent's small room | the right, by your machine |
| **The loop** | one API call per turn | send tube up (back right), reply chute down (back left), the cart | the corners, and the floor |

Nothing else stands in the room. Every object belongs to one zone, so the room explains itself: back = what it
remembers, left = what you control, right = what it can do.

## One turn

Copy of the tray up the tube (right) → reply down the chute (left) onto the pad → words slide along the lip into the
tray and out through your window → if there's an amber card, **the cart** picks it up → the gate (green, ask, or red)
→ a bench runs it → the cart brings the jade tiles back and tips them in at the open end → up again. The cart is the
turn marker: it can roll either way along the front rail, so results reach the open end wherever it is (turn one:
near the left; late: near the right), with no tile ever sliding backwards on its own.

## Fixes to plan-f1 inside this round

- **Seeing the hands:** the right wall is low (waist high, like a counter) with the ports in it, so your files, shell
  and MCP server stand visibly just beyond it; the camera at 16° looks over it. The back and left walls stay tall.
- **Results:** carried by the cart to the open end, not slid leftward along the lip.
- **Send:** one standing glass tube in the back-right corner, drawn the same in plan and section.
- **Writer:** rides the tray's back edge with the open end.
