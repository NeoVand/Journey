- Five-second: "memory, hands, reins" is three nouns to learn, and "reins" needs the horse explained before it lands.
- Facts: "memory" lumps the tray with the record on disk, the one distinction card 5 exists to make; the subagent is not a hand.
- One object: the cart is honest (it is the turn marker given a job); "memory" still holds five objects.
- World: a waist-high right wall changes the room the user asked to keep, and things "just beyond" a low wall are as good as inside.
- Camera: the low wall solves the grazing angle, but by changing the room rather than the picture.
- Cards: all six are served; card 5 is the weakest because record and tray share a zone.
- Delight: the cart, and nothing else.

## The description, in one breath

> The model, far away, can only read and write, and remembers nothing from one turn to the next. The harness is
> the room around it that does the three things it cannot: **it remembers** (one tray holds everything so far),
> **it acts** (what the reply asks for is done on your machine, at a bench by the wall), and **it asks** (every act
> passes one gate, and you hold it). Round and round, until a reply asks for nothing.

Same core as round 1, sharper words: three verbs instead of three nouns, because a newcomer keeps what a thing
*does*, and each verb is one object you can point at in the wide shot: the tray, the benches, the gate. The horse
is still there for whoever wants it (a harness puts a strong pull to work and gives the driver the reins), but the
sentence no longer needs it.

## Three zones, one loop

| It… | Real thing | Objects | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **remembers** | the context, rebuilt and reread every turn | **the tray** (hero), with its end stop; what fills it: the four drawers and the skill shelf in the back wall; what keeps it: the writer riding the open end, its tape to the slot in the back wall (your disk); what squeezes it: the press, which is the end stop | the back |
| **asks** | permission: your rules, the mode, the bell, your hooks | **the gate** (rule rack, mode dial, bell, periscope to a second model), your screen's window beside it, the hook port in the wall | the left, by you |
| **acts** | tools run on your machine | **three benches** against the right wall, each with a cable into a port: your files, your shell (in the glass fence when the sandbox is on), an MCP server; the subagent's small room next to them, inside | the right, by your machine |
| **the loop** | one API call per turn | the send tube in the back-right corner, the reply chute in the back-left corner, the pad and the lip, **the cart** | the corners and the front |

Three verbs, three walls: back = what it remembers, left = what it asks you, right = what it does. The subagent is
"it acts" too: an act that is itself a whole small room, brief in, summary out.

## One turn

A copy of the tray rides the tube up (back right) → the reply comes down the chute (back left) onto the pad →
words slide along the lip to the open end, and a copy goes out through your window → an amber card is a request,
not an act: **the cart** (the turn marker) takes it to the gate → green, bell, or red; red goes back onto the tray
as a red tile and nothing runs → green: the cart rolls to a bench, the bench runs it through its port, jade tiles
come out → the cart brings them back and tips them in at the open end, wherever it has got to → the writer copies
them onto the tape → up again. When a reply has no amber card the cart stops at the open end, and that is the end
of the turn.

The cart rides the front rail both ways: to the gate on the left, to a bench on the right, back to the open end.
No tile ever slides backwards on its own; the harness carries things. That is the one picture to keep: **the model
never moves; the harness runs.**

## What changed from round 1, and why

- **Verbs for nouns.** "Remembers, asks, acts" says what a harness *is for* in words a newcomer already has; "memory,
  hands, reins" asked them to learn a metaphor first. Same zones, same objects.
- **Record and tray pulled apart in words.** "It remembers" is the tray; the writer and the slot are "what keeps it",
  named separately, so card 5 (the record keeps everything, the tray gets the summary) has its two objects.
- **The cart, accepted.** It fixes plan-f1's real fault (results sliding leftward along the lip) and gives the turn
  marker a job. The lip now carries only what comes from the left (words, your prompt, a refusal); the cart carries
  cards out and results back.
- **The tall right wall, kept.** The user asked for the same room. The benches stand on the floor and read from the
  camera; the port is a brass ring with a board (icon and name, as at the gates), the cable goes into the wall and
  stops. Nothing is drawn outside: your files, your shell and MCP servers are *beyond*, which is the fact. If the
  wide shot still wants more, pan the camera to about 8°, not the wall down to the waist.
- **Send as one standing tube, back right.** Plan-f1's capsule from the ceiling was drawn hanging in both views, but
  a standing tube is what the concept renders already show and what the main road can echo; one less novelty.
- **The writer rides the open end**, on the tray's back edge, as round 1 said.
- **Nothing added.** Same objects as round 1; the shelf stays because card 3 needs it.
